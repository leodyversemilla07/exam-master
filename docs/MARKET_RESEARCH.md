# ExamMaster Market Research Documentation

## Overview

This document summarizes market research conducted for ExamMaster pricing validation in the Philippine education sector, focusing on market size, competitor analysis, and willingness to pay (WTP).

## Philippine Education Market Size

Based on data from the Department of Education (DepEd), Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), and related reports:

- **Schools**: Approximately 47,000 public and private schools nationwide (K-12 basic education). Public schools dominate (about 40,000), with private schools (around 7,000) often catering to higher-income segments.
- **Students**: About 28 million in total education (23 million in K-12 basic education, 3.5 million in higher education). Enrollment rate is around 97%, with slight declines in recent years due to economic factors.
- **Teachers**: Around 800,000 educators, including 600,000 in basic education and 200,000 in higher ed. Public school teachers make up the majority.

The market is large but fragmented, with public schools receiving government funding while private ones operate on tuition fees.

## Willingness to Pay

- **Teacher Salaries**: Average ₱20,000/month for public school teachers (PSA data); private school teachers earn ₱15,000-₱30,000. Many supplement income, limiting personal edtech spend to ₱200-₱500/month max.
- **Edtech Adoption Rates**: Low (10-20% of schools use advanced tools), per Philstar reports on edtech growth amid pandemic challenges. Budget constraints in public schools (government-funded) favor free/low-cost tools; private schools invest more but prioritize essentials like textbooks.
- **Constraints**: High poverty rates (20% of population) and economic caution post-pandemic reduce willingness to pay. Teachers prefer free trials and discounts; schools cite limited ICT budgets. However, growing demand for online assessments (post-COVID) increases potential for affordable tools.

## Pricing Recommendations

Current tiers (₱499/₱1,499/₱4,999) were higher than competitors (e.g., Quizlet at ₱220-330/month). Adjusted downward to align with market willingness (teachers afford ₱200-500/month; schools ₱1,000-5,000/year) and boost adoption:

- **₱499 Tier** → Lower to ₱299: Target individual teachers; position as "entry-level" for small classes (up to 50 students), emphasizing free trials to overcome budget hesitancy.
- **₱1,499 Tier** → Adjust to ₱999: For mid-sized use (100-200 students); include school discounts to compete with ClassPoint/Socrative.
- **₱4,999 Tier** → Reduce to ₱2,999: Enterprise for large schools/districts (unlimited users); offer annual plans with 20-30% discounts to match Kahoot! tiers and encourage bulk adoption.

Overall, prioritize annual subscriptions with discounts, free trials, and localization (e.g., Filipino language support) to penetrate the public school market. Monitor edtech growth reports for refinements.
