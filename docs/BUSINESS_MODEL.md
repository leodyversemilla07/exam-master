# ExamMaster Business Model Documentation

## Overview

ExamMaster operates as a **Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) subscription platform** tailored for Philippine educational institutions, providing AI-powered exam creation and Table of Specifications (TOS) generation tools.

## Target Market

- **Primary**: Philippine educational institutions (public schools, private schools, universities, technical/vocational schools)
- **Secondary**: Individual educators, curriculum developers, and education consultants
- **Geographic Focus**: Philippines, with potential expansion to Southeast Asian markets
- **Market Size**: ~47,000 schools (40,000 public, 7,000 private), 28 million students, 800,000 teachers
- **Market Insights**: High adoption potential in public schools due to DepEd initiatives; private schools prioritize quality tools; willingness to pay constrained by budgets (teachers: ₱200-500/month, schools: ₱1,000-5,000/year)

## Business Model Structure

### SaaS Subscription Tiers

All pricing in Philippine Pesos (₱), billed monthly or annually (15% discount for annual plans).

#### 1. Basic Tier (₱299/month)

**Target**: Individual teachers, small schools, trial users

- 500 AI-generated questions per month
- Manual TOS creation
- Basic question bank (up to 100 questions)
- PDF export (exams only)
- Email support
- 1 user account

#### 2. Pro Tier (₱999/month)

**Target**: Medium-sized schools, departments

- Unlimited AI-generated questions
- Automated TOS generation with Bloom's Taxonomy
- Advanced question bank (unlimited questions)
- PDF/Word export with answer keys
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Duplicate detection
- Student portal access (up to 100 students)
- Priority email/chat support
- Up to 5 user accounts

#### 3. Enterprise Tier (₱2,999/month)

**Target**: Large schools, universities, school districts

- All Pro features
- White-label branding
- Advanced analytics and reporting
- LMS integrations (Canvas, Moodle, etc.)
- Custom curriculum templates
- API access for integrations
- Bulk question import/export
- Dedicated account manager
- Phone/video support
- Unlimited users and students

### Additional Services

- **Custom Implementation**: ₱25,000 one-time fee for institution-specific setup
- **Training Workshops**: ₱5,000 per session for educator training
- **Data Migration**: ₱10,000 for importing existing question banks

## Revenue Streams

1. **Subscription Fees**: Primary recurring revenue from tier subscriptions
2. **Professional Services**: Implementation, training, and consulting
3. **Premium Features**: Add-ons like advanced AI models or custom integrations
4. **Partnership Commissions**: Referral fees from LMS platform integrations

## Cost Structure

- **AI API Costs**: ~₱50-200/month per active user (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude)
- **Infrastructure**: ₱100,000-200,000/month (servers, databases, CDN)
- **Development**: ₱200,000/month (maintenance, updates)
- **Marketing**: ₱50,000/month initially, scaling with growth
- **Support**: ₱30,000/month for customer service

## Pricing Strategy

- **Value-Based Pricing**: Reflects time savings and quality improvements for educators
- **Competitive Analysis**: Positioned below international competitors but above local alternatives; adjusted based on market research to align with willingness to pay (competitors like Kahoot! at ₱1,000-₱3,000/month, Quizlet at ₱220-₱330/month)
- **Discounts**: 20% for DepEd-accredited public schools, bulk discounts for >10 accounts; free trials to overcome budget hesitancy

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Market Entry (Months 1-6)

- **Pilot Programs**: Free trials for 3-5 schools to gather feedback
- **Partnerships**: Collaborate with DepEd regional offices
- **Digital Marketing**: Facebook/LinkedIn ads targeting Filipino educators

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 7-18)

- **Content Marketing**: Educational blog posts, webinars on assessment best practices
- **Events**: Exhibit at PhilTESOL, BETT Asia, and local education conferences
- **Referral Program**: 20% commission for successful referrals

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 19+)

- **Enterprise Sales**: Direct outreach to large universities
- **Channel Partners**: Work with education consultants and software resellers
- **International Expansion**: Southeast Asia markets with localized pricing

## Key Success Metrics

- **Customer Acquisition**: 100 active schools in Year 1
- **Retention Rate**: 85% annual retention
- **Revenue Goals**: ₱5M ARR by end of Year 2
- **User Engagement**: Average 200 questions generated per active user monthly

## Risk Mitigation

- **Market Risk**: Diversify across public/private sectors
- **Technical Risk**: Multi-provider AI redundancy (OpenAI + Gemini + Claude)
- **Regulatory Risk**: Ensure DepEd compliance and data privacy (PDPA)
- **Financial Risk**: Bootstrap with conservative burn rate, achieve profitability by Month 12

## Financial Projections

- **Year 1**: ₱2M revenue, ₱500K profit
- **Year 2**: ₱8M revenue, ₱2M profit
- **Year 3**: ₱15M revenue, ₱4M profit

## Implementation Timeline

- **Month 1-2**: Legal setup, payment integration (GCash, Maya)
- **Month 3-4**: Beta testing with pilot schools
- **Month 5**: Official launch with marketing campaign
- **Ongoing**: Feature updates, customer feedback integration
